IV. Strategic Outlook 2025

Moving into the 2025 cycle, the platform will prioritize Cross-Border Academic Exchange. Our data indicates that hybrid volunteering models—combining physical on-site work with remote digital mentorship—generate 1.4x higher long-term impact scores for students.

Key Objectives:

  • Integration of AI-driven impact forecasting.
  • Expansion of the "Global Citizen" scholarship fund.
  • Standardization of Impact Scores across 100% of campus units.
